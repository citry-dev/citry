Error-prevention benchmark

The same 63 developer mistakes, written in each framework where they apply. Each one is counted where it was first reported: an error or a warning before the app runs (editor, check or build), an error or a warning at runtime, or not at all. Earlier is better. Hover or focus a bar part, or a table cell, for its details.

E

C

B

R

M

?

P
the framework's design prevents the mistake
-

n/a

!
the clean app reported problems in this run, so the cell is unreliable
~
a tool timed out or skipped this case, so it may have caught the mistake earlier
°
the report points away from the change, and the case explains why it still counts

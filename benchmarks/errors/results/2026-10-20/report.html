Error-prevention benchmark

The same 24 developer mistakes, written in each framework where they apply. Each one is counted where it was first reported: an error or a warning before the app runs (editor, check or build), an error or a warning at runtime, or not at all. Earlier is better. Hover or focus a bar part, or a table cell, for its details.
